What makes LinkZorven's link building 100% white-hat?
+
We strictly avoid Private Blog Networks (PBNs), automated link-injection tools, web 2.0 spams, and spammy link farms. Every backlink we secure is placed in high-quality, editorial content through genuine manual outreach to real site owners and publishers with verified organic Google traffic.
Are backlinks guaranteed to be dofollow and permanent?
+
Yes. All placements in our Starter, Growth, and Premium packages are guaranteed contextual in-content Dofollow links. We offer a free replacement guarantee if any link is removed or changed by an editor within 6 months of delivery.
Can I approve the websites and anchor texts before publishing?
+
Absolutely! We work in total transparency. We share domain options, metrics (DR, monthly traffic, spam score), and recommended anchor text distributions for your review and sign-off before any article goes live.
How long does it take to see ranking improvements?
+
While Google indexing occurs within a few days, notable ranking spikes typically materialize between 30 to 60 days as link equity and topical trust permeate through your domain. Check our verified client case studies above to see our real Google Search Console timelines!
